# Lila — locked identity sheet

Shared canonical character sheet, used by multiple skills (fumo-plushie,
bot-icon). Each skill applies its own style rules on top and ignores traits
that don't apply to it (e.g. plush-fabric notes for bot icons, capsule-eye
rules for plushies).

Locked 2026-09-28 after three iterations; updated 2026-10-26. Treat everything below as canon for
this character; do not re-derive from a new stylized image without checking
against this sheet first.

Sided vocabulary follows the M1 glossary (adopted 2026-10-26, user-directed):
Port = her left side when facing the bow, Starboard = her right side;
Bow = facing direction (nose/face), Stern = back of the head.
Port/starboard never flip with camera angle; viewer-side equivalents are
given in parentheses for front views.

- **Hair:** short blue-purple bob with bangs sweeping across the forehead to
  half-cover the Port eye (her left eye; viewer's right in a front view).
  Built from chunky soft strands; bangs and side hair as overlapping pieces.
- **Eyes:** large round pale-blue irises — the same pale blue as the halo,
  but matte, not glowing. White stitched highlights, thin dark upper lash
  lines. The Starboard eye (her right eye; viewer's left in a front view)
  is fully visible and wears small round thin-framed glasses. The Port
  eye (her left eye; viewer's right in a front view) is half-covered by
  the bangs.
- **Expression:** small neutral mouth — a single short embroidered curve.
  Faint blush on both cheeks at most.
- **Halo:** pale-blue glowing ring floating above the head. Soft and gently
  luminous, never harsh. Same pale blue as the eyes.
- **Wings:** small white feathered angel wings on the back, layered soft
  feathers.
- **Outfit:** black STRAPLESS double-slit cheongsam (qipao-style) — bare
  shoulders, black fabric starting at the bust with a diagonal wrap neckline,
  high side slits on both sides of the skirt. Wide blue sash with a subtle
  diamond pattern around the waist. Long black detached arm warmers from
  wrist to upper arm. Two thin black buckled armbands on the Port upper
  arm (her left arm; viewer's right in a front view), above the long black
  arm warmer. Visibility depends on perspective and facing: in a back view
  the same arm reads viewer's left, and in side views it may be the far
  arm or occluded. Do not require armbands visible in every frame.
- **Legs/feet:** BARE pale skin showing through the cheongsam's side slits —
  no legwear, no stockings, no tights. Simple bare rounded feet.
- **Props:** none.

## Traps this sheet exists to prevent

- The bangs/glasses sideness: bangs cover the Port eye, glasses on the
  Starboard eye (viewer's right / viewer's left respectively in a front
  view). v1 generated it mirrored; the user corrected it.
- The black top is STRAPLESS with bare shoulders — v2 gave it a mandarin
  collar and shoulder coverage; wrong.
- The black around the legs in the blurry side views is the cheongsam skirt
  with slits, NOT dark legwear — v1 put her in black tights; legs are bare.
- Eye color is pale blue (halo-hued, matte). Do not default to a deeper
  anime blue.
