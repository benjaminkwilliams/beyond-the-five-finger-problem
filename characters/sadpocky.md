# SadPocky — locked identity sheet

Shared canonical character sheet, used by multiple skills (fumo-plushie,
bot-icon). Each skill applies its own style rules on top and ignores traits
that don't apply to it.

Locked 2026-09-26; updated 2026-10-26. Treat everything below as canon for this character; do not
re-derive from a new stylized image without checking against this sheet first.

Sided vocabulary follows the M1 glossary (adopted 2026-10-26, user-directed):
Port = her left side when facing the bow, Starboard = her right side;
Bow = facing direction (nose/face), Stern = back of the head.
Port/starboard never flip with camera angle; viewer-side equivalents are
given in parentheses for front views.

- **Hair:** very long pale MINT-GREEN twin-tails (not white/silver — greenish
  tint) with pink gradient tips and straight bangs; thin dark hair ties at
  the twin-tail bases (subtle, not decorative). NOT braided — smooth,
  loose-flowing twin-tails.
- **Ears:** tall upright rabbit ears — MINT-GREEN outer fur matching her hair
  color (not white, no pink ombré), pink inner ears, small white pom-pom at
  the base of each ear on the FRONT side only (not visible from the rear).
  Real ears, not a headband. The pink is the inner ear (ear hole) and faces
  FORWARD — from behind, the ears read as plain mint-green.
- **Eyes:** dark red eyes, half-lidded, tired/sad look.
- **Expression:** flat, bothered mouth; light pink blush on the cheeks. Sad,
  unimpressed energy — not pouty-cute.
- **Outfit:** cropped white sailor-style school top with dark navy collar
  (white stripe trim) and a large red bow at the chest, midriff showing; navy
  pleated skirt. Worn UNDER an oversized white sukajan-style souvenir jacket
  with pink inner lining, worn open — the jacket has yellow bows on the
  sleeves, dark navy stripes on the sleeves and hem, pink cuffs, and a large
  red 兎 (rabbit) kanji on the back. NOT a varsity/letterman jacket — no
  ribbed striped cuffs or hem band.
- **Lower body (confirmed by ref sheet 2026-09-26):** navy pleated skirt,
  white socks, pink-and-white sneakers.
- **Leg markers, sided — do not mirror or swap (positions locked to source):**
  PortLeg (her left leg; viewer's right in a front view): carrot decal +
  small bunny decal on the upper thigh, pink band-aid on the knee, white
  sock. StarboardLeg (her right leg; viewer's left in a front view):
  black thigh strap with heart ring, green band-aid on the shin. The
  strap is starboard; the decals-section reading governs over any bare
  left/right shorthand.
- **Motifs:** carrot (name branding, "SADPOCKY" logo) and small bunny icons.
- **Props:** none. **Gesture:** none — hands rest as mitten lumps.

## Variants

- **Default:** as the reference sheet shows — cropped top, pleated skirt, no
  visible underwear layer.
- **"Standard equipment":** modest white underwear beneath the pleated skirt,
  fully covering, matching the school-uniform palette. Use when the user
  explicitly asks for the X-safe version.

## Traps this sheet exists to prevent

- Do NOT confuse with Pipkin Pippa (`pipkin-pippa.md`): pink hair, bunny-ear
  headband, magenta eyes, pastel cardigan. Another session misfiled Pippa's
  sheet under the name "sadpocky" on 2026-09-26; corrected the same day.
- The ears are real rabbit ears growing from her head, not a headband.
  Render them upright and tall, not floppy.
- Sided markers use port/starboard, never bare left/right. Bare "left"
  flips meaning between viewer-side and her-side and once misdescribed
  the starboard strap.
